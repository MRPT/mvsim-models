Normal maps (*_Normal.png, *_normal.png) use the OpenGL convention (green
points to the image top). Asphalt01, BrickWall01 and BrickWall02 normal maps
come from cgbookcase (converted from DirectX convention). All others are
generated from the color textures with generate-all-normal-maps.sh.
