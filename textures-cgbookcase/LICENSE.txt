Normal maps (*_Normal.png, *_NormalGL.png, *_normal.png) use the OpenGL
convention (green points to the image top). All *_NormalGL.png normal maps
come from cgbookcase (converted from DirectX convention). All others are
generated from the color textures with generate-all-normal-maps.sh (some of
them are superseded by an official *_NormalGL.png, but kept for older MVSim
versions that still reference them).
